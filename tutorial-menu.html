Tutorial menu.

Every item is a standalone workflow with a clear deliverable you walk away with. For people who don't have a technical background but want to fully utilize AI's potential. Some items are more technical and require more time than others, but each one is labeled with its complexity level.

01

Instant marketing makeover

Beginner

Turn reference images you like + your own product photos into polished, on-brand marketing visuals. Then make them editable and shareable instantly.

Workflow
  • Find social media posts or ads with aesthetics you like
  • Upload those + your product photos into GPT Image 2
  • AI combines the style with your actual product
  • Import into Canva Magic Studio to make the result editable (swap text, adjust layout)
  • Export or share via Canva link (no website or domain needed)
Tools + cost

GPT Image 2 — included in ChatGPT Plus
Canva Magic Studio — included in Canva Pro

~$35/mo (ChatGPT Plus $20 + Canva Pro $15)

You walk away with

5-10 ready-to-post social media visuals and marketing assets

02

Brand system + design system

Beginner-Intermediate

A complete visual brand identity (colors, typography, mood, visual rules) that AI agents can understand and apply automatically to everything you create.

Workflow

  • Build a design system in Claude Design that translates your brand into a format AI can reuse
  • Claude Design reads your brand and applies it consistently to any future project
Tools + cost

GPT Image 2 — included in ChatGPT Plus
Claude Design — included in Claude Pro

~$40/mo (ChatGPT Plus $20 + Claude Pro $20)

You walk away with

A visual brand book + a reusable AI design system

Example brand book (AI-generated)

Why this matters: This is the foundation. Once you have a design system, every presentation, website, or app you create with AI will automatically match your brand without you manually styling anything.

06

Video editing with CapCut

Practical

Turn raw footage into polished short-form videos using templates and AI-assisted editing.

Workflow
  • Shoot raw footage on your phone (product clips, behind-the-scenes, unboxing)
  • CapCut AutoCut / Auto Clipper: AI selects best moments, applies template, adds music and transitions
  • AI image and video generation directly inside CapCut
  • Auto-captions, background removal, voice effects
  • Core editing basics: trimming, transitions, text overlays, audio
Tools + cost

CapCut — free, or Pro for extra features

Free to $9.99/mo

You walk away with

3-5 short-form videos ready for social media

Note: Primarily taught on mobile (phone-first workflow), since that's where most raw footage lives. CapCut also has a desktop version for more detailed editing. Includes many AI features beyond basic editing: auto-captions, AI image/video generation, background removal.

08

Research-to-content pipeline (manual)

Intermediate

Research any topic deeply, turn it into a script, and produce a talking-head video without filming yourself using an AI avatar.

Workflow
  • Upload sources into NotebookLM (articles, competitor sites, industry reports)
  • NotebookLM synthesizes the research into key insights
  • Feed insights to AI to write a video script
  • Create your AI voice clone and avatar on HeyGen (your digital twin that talks and gestures like you)
  • HeyGen generates the talking-head video from your script
  • Add B-roll footage (product shots, lifestyle clips) in CapCut
  • Post to social media
Tools + cost

NotebookLM — free
Claude — included in Claude Pro
HeyGen — free tier for short clips, paid from $24/mo
CapCut — free, or Pro $9.99/mo

Free to ~$54/mo

You walk away with

A complete educational or promotional video, ready to post

09

Research-to-content pipeline (automated)

Advanced

Automate the entire process from Item 8 so AI agents handle most of the work.

Workflow
  • AI agent researches the topic
  • AI agent writes the script
  • AI agent generates the avatar video via HeyGen
  • AI agent cuts in B-roll
  • Content goes into Airtable content calendar
  • Auto-scheduled and posted via Blotato across all platforms
Tools + cost

Claude — included in Claude Pro ($20/mo)
NotebookLM — free
HeyGen — from $24/mo
CapCut — free or Pro $9.99/mo
Airtable — free tier, or Plus $20/seat/mo
Blotato — $29/mo starter

~$93/mo+

You walk away with

A fully automated content production and publishing system

Note: This is the most advanced item and requires more technical setup. Learn the manual version (Item 8) first, then automate each step one at a time. Not everyone will need this level of automation.
