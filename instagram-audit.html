A quick read on @kurajewellery.

Just the signal from what's publicly visible. Not a full audit — we'll talk through the details together. A few observations on the website at the bottom too.

For a 16-year-old brand with strong fundamentals, the follower count is lower than it could be. The biggest tell is the near 1:1 follow ratio — for a luxury brand, this is the one structural thing to fix first. Unfollowing down to under 500 strategic accounts re-positions the feed in the algorithm as a brand worth following, not a peer trying to grow.

A few things that stood out.

The "stack, style, personalize" idea isn't fully shown.

Your whole positioning is layering. That's a Reel format — a hand reaches in, adds rings one by one, the look transforms across 5 seconds. Even a slow carousel that shows the same pieces stacked three different ways would be on-brand.

Your face appears, but only sometimes.

When you do, it lifts the trust signal a lot. A founder-led pattern (even one Reel a month where you talk about a piece you designed) compounds over time, especially with your story — banking VP to GIA to founder.

Virtual try-on and bespoke aren't visible on the feed.

Two of your strongest differentiators on the site. Six short Reels demoing the try-on flow on six bestselling SKUs could become a permanent funnel from IG to consultation bookings.

Pinterest is the quiet asset.

Pinterest sends more cold traffic to jewellery e-commerce than any other social platform. Republishing your IG hero shots there with link-back to product is the highest-ROI low-effort move.

Hashtags are likely too broad.

If you're using #jewelry or #goldjewelry, those are 10M+ post bands — impossible to win. Mid-tail is the play: #18kgoldjewelry, #stackingrings, #vancouverjeweller, #bespokejewellery (50k-500k post bands).

A note on the website.

The product range is genuinely luxury but kurajewellery.com presents at a softer tier than the pieces deserve. Worth a refresh pass to lift the premium feel:

  • Hero photography that uses more negative space and editorial composition (the GPT Image 2 Tier B / Tier D examples in this library are good references for the look)
  • Slower, more confident typography in the hero zone, less density
  • A single signature collection page treated as a campaign, not a grid
  • The founder story page is great content but visually muted — could carry more weight
  • Anti-markup ethos is a real differentiator, but it's buried in body copy. Worth a dedicated page or hero panel.
