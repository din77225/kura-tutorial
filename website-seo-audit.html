Session 1

SEO read on kurajewellery.com.

Crawled the homepage, the all-collections page, a representative product page, and the Our Story page. Tested where Kura ranks for the queries that matter. Healthy at the technical layer, missing at the content layer, and losing on the queries where it should be winning.

Where Kura ranks well.

"kura jewellery" — position 1

Branded search owns the SERP, as it should. Both the main domain and the IG account rank in the top three.

"18k gold cage necklace bespoke" — position 2

Mid-tail query Kura should own, and largely does. Only AUVERE outranks. This is a category Kura can defend with more depth — a dedicated /collections/cage-collection page with proper internal links would push toward position 1.

Where Kura is losing.

"alphabet diamond necklace 18k gold buy online" — not in top 10

Despite the Juliet Alphabet Diamonds collection being a clear bestseller, Kura isn't appearing on a high-intent query they sell directly into. Gorjana, Senco, CaratLane, Auraten, and even Tiffany rank above. The fix is on-page: the current collection page title is generic, the H1 doesn't include the keyword phrase, and there's no schema markup signaling the products to Google.

"stackable rings 18k gold canada online" — not in top 10

Kura is based in Vancouver but isn't ranking for Canadian stackable-ring searches. Mejuri, Blue Ruby (Vancouver competitor), and Pandora dominate. The "stack, style, personalize" positioning is the whole brand and the site isn't being credited for it because there's no dedicated "stackable rings" landing page, no Canadian geo-tagging, and no local schema.

Technical SEO findings.

No JSON-LD product schema visible.

This is the biggest single fix on the site. Without Product, Offer, AggregateRating, and BreadcrumbList structured data, Google can't pull Kura products into rich results — no price, no star rating, no availability shown in the SERP. Every Shopify theme has a schema toggle or can take a 30-line snippet in theme.liquid. Worth one developer afternoon.

Title tag patterns are weak.

Examples found: "Shop Signature Cage Necklace (No Diamonds) on Oval Long Chain in 18K Gold Online" (starts with "Shop", ends with "Online", brand name absent). And "Kura Jewellery - Our Story | Kura Jewellery" (brand name written twice). Strong pattern: {Product Name} in 18K Gold | Kura Fine Jewellery for products, {Page} | Kura Fine Jewellery for pages. Easy site-wide fix in Shopify theme settings.

Meta descriptions are missing or auto-generated.

Not visible in source on the pages tested. Google falls back to the first body text, which on the homepage starts with "Discover a world of Kura Jewellery" — no keywords, no value prop, no CTA. Every collection and product page should have a hand-written 150-character meta description with the keyword plus the anti-markup angle.

Heading structure is OK on the homepage, weak on Our Story.

Homepage uses one H1, multiple H2s. Good. Our Story uses "WHO ? WHEN ? WHERE ?" and "WHAT IS IT ABOUT ?" as H2s. Search engines de-weight non-descriptive headers. Replace with the actual keywords ("Founded in Hong Kong, 2009", "How Kura is priced", "Where Kura jewellery is made").

Sitemap is healthy.

Standard Shopify five-sitemap setup including an agentic-discovery sitemap (newer Shopify feature, good). No action needed.

Robots.txt is fine.

Standard Shopify with a Shopify-added clause about agent checkout flows. Doesn't affect Google or any normal crawler. Leave as-is.

Content gaps.

No blog content active.

A blog sitemap exists but the content isn't competing on the queries Kura should own. Four evergreen posts would move the needle: "How to stack necklaces without tangling", "18K vs 14K vs gold-filled: what's actually different", "Fair pricing in fine jewellery: what you're really paying for", "What to ask before commissioning a bespoke piece". Each is on-brand, ranks for high-intent searches, and feeds the bespoke funnel.

No dedicated Vancouver / local presence.

Erny is in Vancouver, the brand has Vancouver customers, but the site doesn't signal local. A simple "Visit us in Vancouver" or "Bespoke consultations in Vancouver" landing page with local schema (LocalBusiness, address, hours) opens an entire category of Vancouver-jeweller searches.

Off-site reputation is thin.

Judge.me reviews are on-site and strong, but Trustpilot shows essentially zero verified reviews. Encouraging existing happy customers to leave Trustpilot and Google Business reviews would raise the social-proof signal that Google factors into local rankings.

A note on what's not in this audit.

Keyword position tracking, backlink profile, organic traffic estimates, and competitor-share-of-voice all need a paid tool (Ahrefs, SEMrush, or similar) to measure properly. The findings above come from live SERP testing, on-page inspection, and public sitemaps. If you want a deeper data layer at some point, a single Ahrefs site audit run gives you the rest in one report.
