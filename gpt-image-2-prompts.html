How to use these.

The prompts work copy-paste, but you'll get better results with a quick rhythm. After we walk through a few together, this is the loop you can run on your own:

  • Decide what kind of post you need this week and find the matching tier above.
  • Copy a prompt, swap in details that fit a Kura piece (metal, stone, finish, mood).
  • Run it. Start with one render, not five — see how close it lands first.
  • Refine in Canva Magic Layer: clean up the metal edges, swap a background, fix any awkward shadows.
  • Save the prompts that worked into a small library of your own — same prompt structure works across new pieces.
  • The example image on each card is your aesthetic target. If your output looks too far off, the prompt needs a stronger lighting word or composition word.

Tip — keep --ar 4:5 on the end for IG feed, swap to --ar 9:16 for Reels covers and Stories.
